Privacy Policy

Venbuk  ·  Effective date: 1 May 2026  ·  Last updated: 29 April 2026

Short version: Venbuk collects only your email address for login purposes. We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, or use it for marketing. If you delete your account, your data is deleted too.

1. Who We Are

Venbuk is a web application operated as a personal project, based in Zambia. For any privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact us at privacy@venbuk.com.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with access to Venbuk:

Email address — collected when you create an account, used solely for authentication (logging in and account management). We do not collect your name, location, phone number, payment details, or any other personal information.

We may also collect basic, non-identifiable usage data (such as how many times certain features are used in aggregate) to understand how the application is being used. This data cannot be traced back to any individual user.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used only to:

— Identify your account and allow you to log in.
— Send account-related messages if necessary (e.g. password resets).

We do not use your email address for marketing, newsletters, or any other purpose.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your email address and login credentials are stored securely. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. We retain your data only for as long as your account is active.

5. Data Deletion

If you choose to delete your Venbuk account, your email address and all associated login details will be permanently deleted from our systems. You can request account deletion at any time by contacting us at privacy@venbuk.com.

6. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. We do not use any advertising networks or marketing platforms that would receive your data.

7. Cookies

Venbuk may use essential cookies or session tokens strictly to keep you logged in to your account. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party analytics cookies that collect personal data.

8. Analytics

If we introduce usage analytics in the future, we will update this policy before doing so. Any analytics we use will be aggregate and non-identifiable — meaning no data will be linked to you personally.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR (EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to access — You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Right to rectification — You can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
Right to erasure — You can ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
Right to restriction — You can ask us to limit how we use your data.
Right to object — You can object to certain types of processing.
Right to data portability — You can request your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@venbuk.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, the right to request deletion of your personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

We do not sell personal information. To submit a privacy request, contact us at privacy@venbuk.com.

11. Children's Privacy

Venbuk is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their email address, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. International Users

Venbuk is operated from Zambia. If you are accessing the application from outside Zambia, please be aware that your data may be processed in a different jurisdiction. By using Venbuk, you consent to this transfer and processing.

13. Changes to This Policy

If we make any meaningful changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of Venbuk after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests relating to this privacy policy or your personal data, please contact us at: privacy@venbuk.com
